# Blue Street — the vision

This is the "why" behind Blue Street. Every agent reads it before adding,
changing or trimming anything. **Only Rami changes this file.** Agents may
suggest an edit in `COLLAB.md`, never make one here.

## What Blue Street is
Rami's FX macro desk: a site that explains what's driving the major
currencies, gold and oil, and where the story is heading, in plain words.
Personal tool first; later a free public site; one day something good enough
that people would pay for it. The benchmark is forexfundamentals.com, and the
goal is to be better than it: match its data sources first, then find better
ones.

## What it must do for the reader
- **A five-minute read.** Open it, know what you're looking at, and know what
  changed. It should feel alive, with depth underneath for whoever wants it.
- **Meaning before numbers.** Every number, event or print runs the four
  beats: what it was, what happened, what it means and why, and what it
  indicates for the currency. Short plain sentences, never "data slop".
- **Makes sense to someone who's been away.** Every headline, card and
  chapter reads on its own for a casual visitor who hasn't opened the site in
  a while: what happened, the bit of background needed to follow it, and
  where things go from here. Friendly, not hand-holding. Plain everyday words,
  not trader clichés like "next test".
- **Evidence, not a verdict.** No house view and no trade recommendations. The
  site builds the case and weighs the evidence (pillars are fine for that);
  Rami renders the verdict.
- **Self-explanatory pages.** No "how to read this" sections. Definitions and
  explainers are always on, with no toggle, and must not clutter the page.
- **Simple navigation.** Five tabs: Today, Currencies, Drivers, Calendar, News.
  Don't add tabs readers have to tell apart.

## When it updates (built around how Rami trades)
Rami swing-trades off the daily and 4-hour charts and doesn't enter trades
late in the New York session. The site is ready *by* his three check-ins:
- **2:00 AM CT — London open:** what happened overnight and what's ahead.
- **8:30 AM CT — New York open:** what the morning's big news and 7:30 AM US
  data did, before the New York session gets going.
- **4:00 PM CT — New York close:** what the day did.
- **Fed days:** a preview at 8:30 and the full story by 4:00, updated quietly
  in between. It shouldn't be in his face, but it should be there if he's in
  a trade.

against this file:
1. If it serves something above, or is listed under "Paused", keep it.
2. If it's clearly finished, replaced or unused, **archive it** (notes go to
   `docs/COLLAB-ARCHIVE.md` word for word); don't silently delete.
3. Deleting a file or a feature needs Rami's yes.
4. If unsure, ask in `COLLAB.md` or ask Rami.
